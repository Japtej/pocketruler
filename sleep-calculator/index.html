The Science of 90-Minute Sleep Cycles

Sleep isn't a uniform state of rest. Throughout the night, human brains cycle through distinct stages of light sleep, deep slow-wave sleep, and REM (dreaming) sleep. Each complete cycle lasts roughly 90 minutes. Waking up in the middle of deep sleep triggers "sleep inertia"—that heavy, groggy feeling that can ruin your morning. By timing your sleep in 90-minute increments, you wake up at the end of a cycle during light sleep feeling refreshed.
