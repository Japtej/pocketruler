Our Story & Mission

Democratizing Financial Clarity for Independent Solopreneurs & Remote Professionals

PocketRuler.app was created with a clear purpose: to solve the mathematical blindspots that hold back the independent economy. From helping freelancers price sustainable client retainers to empowering remote workers to evaluate international expat tax arbitrage across 85+ global cities, we build tools that deliver financial freedom.

Zero Paywalls, Forever Free

We do not believe in gating essential pricing mathematics behind monthly subscriptions, credit cards, or spammy email lists. Every calculator is 100% free and instantly usable.

Client-Side Privacy

Your income, expenses, and client rates are your business. Calculations happen client-side in your web browser. We never log or sell your confidential financial numbers.

Rigorous Formulas

Our formulas account for real-world freelancing realities: self-employment taxes, unbillable marketing hours, operational overhead, and emergency buffer reserves.

Our Methodology & Mathematical Rigor

Corporate salary division (dividing an annual salary by 2,080 hours) fails disastrously for independent contractors. Employees receive paid sick leave, company-matched healthcare, equipment, employer-paid payroll taxes, and guaranteed 40-hour compensation regardless of client acquisition cycles.

To protect freelancers, PocketRuler applies an inverse gross-up model:

Hourly Rate = [ (Net Take-Home ÷ (1 - Tax Rate)) + Business Overhead ] ÷ (1 - Safety Margin) ÷ (Weekly Billable Hours × 48 Working Weeks)

This ensures that your target take-home pay is fully preserved even after paying local taxes, tool subscriptions, and funding your emergency runway.
