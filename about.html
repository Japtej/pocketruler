Our Story & Philosophy

Online tools without the clutter, popups, or paywalls.

We built PocketRuler out of frustration. Every time we needed to calculate something simple—like project pricing, moving expenses, or everyday math—we kept landing on clunky websites from 2004 covered in auto-playing video ads and newsletter popups. We wanted something clean, modern, and fast.

Zero Accounts, Forever Free

You shouldn't have to create an account or hand over your email address just to calculate some numbers. Every tool is ready to use the second you open the page.

Runs Right in Your Browser

Your numbers never leave your device. All calculations run locally on your phone or laptop. We don't save your inputs, track your private finances, or send your data to any server.

Practical, Real-World Math

We build tools for real-life decisions. For example, our freelance calculator factors in unpaid admin hours and taxes, and our relocation tool models actual expat tax rules rather than generic averages.

The Idea: A Digital Pocket Knife for Everyday Life

Think of PocketRuler as the utility belt you keep in your browser bookmarks. When you need to estimate project fees, compare cost of living between cities, or quickly crunch numbers, it's right there—fast, responsive, and completely out of your way.

Our active and planned tools include:

  • Freelance Rate & Retainer Calculator: Figure out sustainable hourly rates, day rates, and retainer packages while accounting for taxes and overhead.
  • Expat Tax & Cost of Living Calculator: Compare net income, local taxes, rent, and monthly savings across 85+ global cities or custom destinations.
  • Everyday Utilities & Converters: Milestone estimators, runway planners, and everyday math helpers.

How PocketRuler stays free: We run unobtrusive display ads on the site to pay for hosting and development. That allows us to keep all tools 100% free for everyone, without subscriptions, paywalls, or selling user data.
