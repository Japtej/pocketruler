Understanding Marketplace Hidden Costs

Side hustles are booming, whether selling handcrafted goods on Etsy, freelancing on Fiverr, or renting properties on Airbnb. However, many beginners make the fatal mistake of looking only at gross revenue. When a marketplace takes a 10% commission, payment gateways take 2.9%, materials cost 20%, and taxes claim another 20%, your actual take-home profit can be half of what you expected.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do marketplace platform fees impact side hustle profits?

Platforms take commissions ranging from 6.5% to 25%, making fee awareness essential for pricing your products or services correctly.

Should I account for self-employment taxes?

Yes, always set aside 15% to 30% for federal, state, and self-employment taxes to prevent surprise tax bills at year-end.
