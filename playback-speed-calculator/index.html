Accelerating Information Consumption

Human speech is typically spoken at 130 to 160 words per minute, while the human brain can comfortably process comprehended speech at up to 350 words per minute. Listening to audiobooks, podcasts, and educational lectures at 1.5x or 2.0x speed not only saves hundreds of hours annually but often improves cognitive focus and retention by eliminating daydreaming lags.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is playback speed time savings calculated?

Actual Listening Time equals Original Duration divided by Speed. Time Saved equals Original Duration minus Actual Listening Time.
