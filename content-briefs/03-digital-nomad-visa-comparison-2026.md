- Renewal: 1yr → 2yr → NO PR path (elective residency only)

- Income: €3,500/mo (2026) — increased from €3,000
- Tax: 50% income tax exemption for 7 years (NEW 2026) — effective ~22% top rate
- Renewal: 2yr → 7yr PR eligible
