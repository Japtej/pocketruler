## Methodology Appendix (for transparency)
- Data collection period: Jan 2024 – Aug 2026
- Platforms: Upwork (public job posts), Toptal (published rates), Gun.io (blog data), Levels.fyi (contractor reports), Stack Overflow Survey 2024
- Normalization: All rates converted to USD 2026 using BLS CPI
- Outlier removal: Top/bottom 5% trimmed per role
- COL adjustment: Numbeo Cost of Living Index (rent excluded for remote)
- Sample sizes: Designer n=4,200; Developer n=8,200
- Confidence: 95% CI ±$8-12/hr depending on role
