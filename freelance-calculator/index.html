Strategy Guide

How to Structure & Sell 3-Tier Client Retainers

Stop selling isolated hours. Move clients into predictable, value-anchored monthly tiers that protect your calendar.

Tier 1 • Advisory

Audit & Advisory Retainer

For clients with internal staff who need expert oversight, architectural reviews, and roadmap guidance without daily task execution.

  • • 5 – 8 hours reserved / month
  • • Bi-weekly 45-min strategy call
  • • Asynchronous code / design review
Typical: $1,200 – $2,000 /mo
Most Popular
Tier 2 • Execution

Dedicated Sprint Retainer

The sweet spot for most solopreneurs. Guarantees steady, sprint-based deliverable execution for a primary client while leaving room for life balance.

  • • 15 – 25 hours reserved / month
  • • Guaranteed 48-hour SLA response
  • • 5% loyalty incentive applied
Typical: $2,800 – $4,500 /mo
Tier 3 • Fractional

Fractional Lead Retainer

You function as an embedded fractional team lead, handling end-to-end technical or creative execution alongside executive leadership.

  • • 30 – 40 hours reserved / month
  • • Same-day urgent slack access
  • • Quarterly strategic roadmap planning
Typical: $5,500 – $9,000+ /mo

How to Calculate Your True Freelance Rate

Why traditional corporate salary math leads to contractor burnout and undercharging.

1

Unbillable Hours

Freelancers only spend 50% to 70% of working hours on paid deliverables. The rest is spent on admin, pitches, and operations.

2

Self-Employment Tax

Independent contractors pay both employer and employee portions of payroll taxes. Always reserve 25% to 35% for taxes off the top.

3

Retainers = Freedom

Retainers smooth erratic cash flow into guaranteed monthly income, eliminating unpaid dry spells and endless client pitching.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the mathematical formula for a freelance hourly rate?

Formula: Hourly Rate = [(Take-Home / (1 - Tax Rate)) + Expenses] / (1 - Buffer Margin) ÷ Annual Billable Hours. Annual billable hours equal weekly billable hours multiplied by 48 working weeks.

How do you convince an hourly client to switch to a retainer?

Frame the retainer around guaranteed capacity and speed: clients get reserved calendar bandwidth, a guaranteed 48-hour response SLA, and a 5% loyalty incentive in exchange for predictable monthly billing.
