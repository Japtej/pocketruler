Methodology, Mathematical Models & Data Governance

We believe cross-border financial decisions require total mathematical transparency, peer-reviewed data feeds, and rigorous editorial ethics.

Our Mission

RelocateTrue was founded by a collective of veteran remote engineers, international tax researchers, and financial economists who experienced firsthand the informational chaos of international relocation.

Existing cost-of-living aggregators routinely fail remote workers by calculating costs solely through the lens of short-term tourists or by omitting the single largest financial expense anyone incurs: taxes and social security contributions. Earning $120,000 remotely in a high-tax European city without a special expat scheme looks radically different than earning that same salary in Dubai or Canggu.

Our mission is to provide an unbiased, 100% free, mathematically transparent simulation platform that models the complete fiscal and lifestyle equation of international remote work.

Primary Data Sources

Our dataset is updated bi-annually and cross-referenced against multiple authoritative statistical and governmental databases:

Cost of Living & Housing

Aggregated from verified crowd-sourced consumer price indices (Numbeo, Expatistan) reconciled against official municipal housing registries, local classified portals (Idealista, Rightmove, Immobiliare), and coworking network directories.

Statutory Tax Brackets

Derived directly from statutory tax legislation and guidance issued by national tax authorities, including the US Internal Revenue Service (IRS), UK HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), Spanish Agencia Tributária (AEAT), Portuguese Autoridade Tributária, and Singapore Inland Revenue (IRAS).

Nomad Visas & Income Thresholds

Audited against official government gazettes, foreign ministry decrees, and consular application portals. We track exact minimum gross monthly salary mandates, application fees, and statutory validity periods.

Foreign Exchange & PPP

Spot exchange rates and Purchasing Power Parity conversion factors sourced from the International Monetary Fund (IMF), World Bank International Comparison Program (ICP), and European Central Bank (ECB).

Mathematical Formulation & Logic

We do not believe in proprietary black-box calculations. Below is the exact mathematical formulation executing inside our zero-dependency JavaScript engine:

// 1. Effective Income Tax Model
TaxableIncome = max(0, GrossAnnualIncome - StandardDeduction)
BaseTax = ∑(BracketAmount_i × BracketRate_i)
TotalTaxAnnual = BaseTax + (GrossAnnualIncome × StateTaxRate) + min(GrossAnnualIncome, Cap) × SocialRate
EffectiveTaxRate = TotalTaxAnnual / GrossAnnualIncome
// 2. True Living Expenses Model
MonthlyLivingCost = Rent(HousingTier) + (Utilities × UtilMultiplier) + (Groceries × LifestyleMult) + (Dining × DiningMult) + (Transit × TransitMult)
// 3. Monthly Discretionary Surplus
NetTakeHomeMonthly = (GrossAnnualIncome - TotalTaxAnnual) / 12
MonthlyDiscretionary = NetTakeHomeMonthly - MonthlyLivingCost
// 4. The Arbitrage Metric
ArbitrageSurplusMonthly = DestinationDiscretionary - OriginDiscretionary
AnnualExtraSavings = ArbitrageSurplusMonthly × 12
RunwayMonthsBanked = max(0, DestinationDiscretionaryAnnual) / DestinationMonthlyLivingCost
