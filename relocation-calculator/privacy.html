Legal & Privacy Compliance

Privacy Policy & Cookies Disclosure

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 • Last Revised: January 15, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Google AdSense Policies

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

At RelocateTrue ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), we respect your privacy. As a web-based financial simulation utility, our core calculations execute entirely client-side within your web browser using vanilla JavaScript. We do not require account registration, login credentials, social security numbers, or sensitive financial documents. Your entered salary and city selections are never stored on external database servers.

2. Information We Collect

A. Client-Side Input Data: Information you input into the calculator (such as annual salary, origin city, destination city, housing preference, and lifestyle multiplier) is processed purely in your browser's local memory to compute real-time mathematical formulas. If you choose to share a custom link, these parameters are serialized into the URL query string (?gross=...&origin=...).

B. Automatically Collected Technical Information: Like most web applications, our web hosting providers (Cloudflare / Vercel) automatically log standard HTTP request metadata, including your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, referring URL, and timestamp for server defense and DDoS mitigation.

C. Direct Inquiries: If you voluntarily reach out via our contact form or editorial email, we receive your name, email address, and message contents solely for responding to your inquiry.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

This website displays advertisements served by Google AdSense and affiliated third-party ad networks to subsidize operational infrastructure and research costs.

• DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on RelocateTrue. Google’s use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

• Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on RelocateTrue and are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

• Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.

4. FTC Affiliate Marketing Disclosure

In accordance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines (16 CFR § 255.5), please be advised that RelocateTrue features contextual affiliate links to select service providers (such as SafetyWing for expat medical insurance, Wise for multi-currency money transfers, and NordVPN for digital cybersecurity).

If you click on an affiliate link and complete an account registration or purchase, RelocateTrue may receive a modest referral commission at absolutely zero additional cost to you. Our editorial reviews, city cost benchmarks, and tax bracket calculations remain strictly independent and are never dictated by affiliate relationships.

5. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

For individuals residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, you possess statutory rights under the GDPR:

  • The right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data we hold.
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw cookie consent at any time through your browser settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@relocatetrue.com.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, request deletion of personal information, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

We do not sell personal information. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google) may collect data through cookies as detailed above. You may exercise your opt-out rights via the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser or through the opt-out links listed in Section 3.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

RelocateTrue does not knowingly address or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our application and financial guides are designed exclusively for adult working professionals, expats, and digital nomads.

8. Amendments to this Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological updates, regulatory changes, or modifications to our advertising integrations. The date of the latest modification is always noted at the top of this document.
