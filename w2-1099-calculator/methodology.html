Our Mission

PocketRuler's W-2 to 1099 conversion engine was created by fintech product veterans, tax researchers, and compensation strategists who experienced firsthand the informational gap when evaluating contractor vs. employee offers.

Existing salary converters routinely fail professionals by using a single "multiply by 1.5" heuristic that ignores the seven distinct cost categories contractors absorb: self-employment tax, unsubsidized health insurance, lost retirement matching, zero paid leave, equity forfeiture, business overhead, and income volatility. These are not rounding errors — they shift the real multiplier from 1.4x to 2.2x depending on your state, seniority, and benefits package.

Our mission is to provide an unbiased, 100% free, mathematically transparent calculator that models the complete fiscal equation of W-2 to 1099 conversion with authoritative 2026 data.

Primary Data Sources

Our dataset is updated annually (tax year) and cross-referenced against multiple authoritative governmental and statistical databases:

Federal Tax Brackets & SE Tax

IRS Revenue Procedure 2024-40 (2026 inflation adjustments): tax brackets, standard deductions, SE tax wage base ($168,600), Additional Medicare Tax threshold ($200k single / $250k MFJ). TCJA provisions assumed extended.

State Income Tax Rates

2026 marginal brackets for all 50 states + DC, sourced from state Departments of Revenue / Franchise Tax Boards. Includes local/city taxes (NYC, Philadelphia, etc.) where applicable. Single filer brackets used.

Health Insurance Premiums

ACA Marketplace 2026 Benchmark Silver Plan premiums by state and age band (30, 40, 50, 60), sourced from CMS public use files and KFF analysis. Employer subsidy baseline: 83% single / 73% family (KFF 2024 Employer Health Benefits Survey).

Retirement & Benefits Benchmarks

BLS National Compensation Survey (2024): 401k match prevalence (98% of large employers), median match 4.5%, vesting schedules. 401k limits: $23,000 + $7,500 catch-up (50+). IRA limits: $7,000 + $1,000 catch-up.

Paid Leave & Equity Norms

BLS: 11 days PTO (1 yr), 15 days (5 yr), 18 days (10 yr), 20 days (20 yr) for private industry. Equity: Levels.fyi / Carta data for refresh grants by level/company stage.

Overhead & Volatility Data

Freelance industry surveys (Upwork, Freelancers Union, MBO Partners): typical overhead $3-8k/yr (tools, legal, accounting, insurance). Income volatility: 15% median gap time between contracts for mid-level; 25%+ for junior.

Mathematical Formulation & Logic

We do not believe in proprietary black-box calculations. Below is the exact mathematical formulation executing inside our zero-dependency JavaScript engine:

// 1. Federal Income Tax (2026 Brackets, Single Filer)
TaxableIncome = max(0, GrossW2Salary - StandardDeduction_2026)
StandardDeduction_2026 = 14,600
FederalTax = Σ(BracketWidth_i × BracketRate_i) for each bracket
// 2. State Income Tax
StateTax = TaxableIncome × StateMarginalRate
// For states with brackets: same progressive calc as federal
// Local taxes (NYC, PHL, etc.) added as separate line item
// 3. FICA (Employee Side) — Already in W-2
FICA_Employee = min(GrossW2Salary, WageBase_2026) × 0.0765
WageBase_2026 = 168,600
AdditionalMedicare = max(0, GrossW2Salary - 200000) × 0.009
// 4. Self-Employment Tax (Contractor Side — FULL 15.3%)
SE_Tax_Base = min(ContractorNetEarnings, WageBase_2026)
SE_Tax = SE_Tax_Base × 0.153 + max(0, ContractorNetEarnings - WageBase_2026) × 0.029
// Deduction for employer half (above-the-line): SE_Tax_Base × 0.0765
// 5. Health Insurance Cost Delta
EmployerSubsidyRate = 0.83 (single) / 0.73 (family) // KFF benchmark
EmployeePremium = BenchmarkSilverPremium(State, Age, Tier) × (1 - EmployerSubsidyRate)
ContractorPremium = BenchmarkSilverPremium(State, Age, Tier) // Full premium
HealthDeltaAnnual = ContractorPremium - EmployeePremium
// 6. 401(k) Match Loss
MatchRate = UserInput (default 0.045) // 4.5% median
MatchCap = UserInput (default 0.06) // Up to 6% of salary
MatchLossAnnual = min(GrossW2Salary × MatchRate, GrossW2Salary × MatchCap)
// 7. Paid Leave Value
PTO_Days = UserInput (default by tenure: 11/15/18/20)
DailyRate = GrossW2Salary / 260 // Working days/year
PTO_Value = PTO_Days × DailyRate
// 8. Equity Refresh (Optional)
EquityAnnualValue = UserInput // From Levels.fyi / offer letter
// 9. Business Overhead
OverheadAnnual = UserInput (default 5000) // Tools, legal, accounting, insurance
// 10. Income Volatility Buffer
VolatilityRate = UserInput (default 0.15) // 15% of contractor revenue
VolatilityBuffer = ContractorGrossRevenue × VolatilityRate
// 11. Total Contractor Cost (Employer Perspective = Your Rate Floor)
ContractorCostAnnual = GrossW2Salary
+ (SE_Tax - FICA_Employee - AdditionalMedicare) // SE tax delta
+ HealthDeltaAnnual
+ MatchLossAnnual
+ PTO_Value
+ EquityAnnualValue
+ OverheadAnnual
+ VolatilityBuffer
// 12. Billable Hours Adjustment
BillableRatio = UserInput (default: 0.60 junior, 0.65 mid, 0.70 senior, 0.75 expert)
AnnualBillableHours = 2080 × BillableRatio
BreakEvenHourlyRate = ContractorCostAnnual / AnnualBillableHours
// 13. Effective Multiplier
W2_Hourly = GrossW2Salary / 2080
Multiplier = BreakEvenHourlyRate / W2_Hourly
