The True Cost of Car Ownership

For urban and suburban residents, owning a personal car is often assumed to be a mandatory lifestyle expense. However, when you aggregate monthly loan payments, comprehensive insurance, fuel, maintenance, municipal parking, and vehicle depreciation, the true monthly cost frequently exceeds $800 to $1,200. In many cities, replacing car ownership with a combination of public transit and strategic ridesharing saves thousands of dollars annually.

What are the hidden costs of car ownership?

Beyond monthly payments and fuel, car ownership includes insurance, parking fees, routine maintenance, repairs, and rapid asset depreciation.
