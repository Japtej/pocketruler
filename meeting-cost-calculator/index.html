The Hidden Price Tag of Corporate Meetings

Meetings are often treated as free because conference rooms don’t send monthly invoices. However, every minute spent in a meeting represents direct salary expenditure paid by the organization. When six senior leaders earning $50/hour gather for an hour-long sync that lacks an agenda, the company invests $300 in labor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the meeting cost ticker calculated?

Cost per second equals (Attendees × Hourly Wage) ÷ 3600, ticking upward second by second in real time.

How can teams reduce meeting overhead?

Enforce strict agendas, limit attendees to essential decision-makers, and replace status update meetings with asynchronous written updates.
